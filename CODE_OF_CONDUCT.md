# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone preparing for CCAR-F can ask a basic question, propose a practice item, or say "I think this note is wrong" without worrying about how they will be received.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Assuming good faith, especially across languages and time zones
- Giving feedback on the work, with a reason and, where possible, a suggested fix
- Accepting correction gracefully — including when it comes from someone with less experience
- Remembering that a "beginner question" from someone new is exactly the signal that a note needs to be clearer

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Dismissing someone's contribution on the basis of who they are rather than what they wrote
- Anything else that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainers directly. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of anyone who reports an incident.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when they do. Sustained or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
