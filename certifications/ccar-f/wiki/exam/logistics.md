# Logistics

Registration, exam-day rules, and policies. Sourced from the official exam guide; **verify current details on the Anthropic Partner Academy and Pearson VUE before you book**, since policies change more often than blueprints.

## Who this exam is for

The guide describes the ideal candidate as a solution architect with roughly **6+ months of hands-on experience** building with the Claude API, the Agent SDK, Claude Code, and MCP — someone who understands both the capabilities and the limitations of large language models in production.

That framing is a warning about item style. Questions are grounded in realistic production situations drawn from actual customer use cases, and they test **judgment about tradeoffs**, not recall of documentation. If you have never watched an agent misroute a tool call in production, the material in [`../heuristics/`](../heuristics/) is the fastest substitute.

## Registration

1. Go to the CCAR-F certification page on the **Anthropic Partner Academy** and review the exam details.
2. Download the Exam Guide and read the **Certification Terms and Conditions** and the **Certification Exam Policy** before registering.
3. Register and complete checkout. The fee shown reflects any discount for your partner tier. List price is **$125 USD**.
4. Follow the confirmation instructions to create your **Pearson VUE** account, then sign in to schedule.
5. Choose a date and either **online proctoring** or a **Pearson test centre**. Delivery is by Pearson VUE.

## Rescheduling and cancellation

You may cancel or reschedule up to **24 hours before** your appointment. Changes inside 24 hours **forfeit the fee**.

**No-show or late arrival** past the permitted window: fee forfeited, and you must re-register.

## Identification

You must present a valid, unexpired, **government-issued photo ID**. The name on it must match your registration **exactly**.

This is a common and entirely avoidable failure. If your registration name needs correcting, contact `certifications-support@anthropic.com` **before scheduling** — not on exam day.

## Accommodations

Reasonable accommodations are available for candidates with documented disabilities or needs, in accordance with applicable law.

Accommodations are requested and approved through **Pearson VUE**, at `pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-takers/accommodations`. **Do not schedule your appointment until your request has been approved** — scheduling first can force a reschedule.

## Exam-day rules

Whether online or at a test centre:

- Stay within view of the proctor and webcam for the entire session, if testing online
- Keep your workspace clear of notes, books, phones, secondary monitors, and any other materials
- Do not communicate with anyone during the exam
- Do not capture, copy, photograph, or reproduce any exam content in any form

Prohibited: mobile phones, smart watches, headphones, study materials, any recording device. Permitted items — such as scratch paper supplied by the proctor — are specified by Pearson VUE.

**Consequences of misconduct** are severe: invalidation of your result, revocation of your credential, and a ban from future exams.

### Practical notes for online proctoring

- Clear your desk completely before the check-in scan, including the second monitor cable if the monitor itself must be removed from view.
- Close everything before check-in; the system check is fussier than you expect and check-in time is not exam time, but a failed check can cost your slot.
- No headphones means no noise cancelling. Pick a genuinely quiet room.

## Confidentiality and NDA

Before the exam begins you must accept a **confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement**. By accepting, you agree that all exam content — questions, answer options, and scenarios — is Anthropic's confidential and proprietary property, and that you will not disclose, reproduce, or distribute any part of it.

If you decline, the session ends and **no refund is issued**.

This obligation continues after the exam. It is why this repository contains no exam content and why [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../../../CONTRIBUTING.md) refuses contributions from people describing what they saw. See [`DISCLAIMER.md`](../../../../DISCLAIMER.md).

## Appeals

You may appeal a decision within **14 days** of being notified of it, or — for a concern about your exam result — within **14 days of your exam date**. Submit appeals to Pearson VUE support.

Two things are **not** appealable: the standard-setting outcome (the 720 cut score) and the content of individual exam items.
