## No exam content

This repository contains **no exam content**.

Candidates accept a confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement before the exam begins. Under that agreement all exam content — questions, answer options, and scenarios encountered during the exam — is the confidential and proprietary property of Anthropic.

Accordingly, this project:

- contains **no** live exam questions, answer options, or item text;
- does **not** reproduce the sample questions printed in the official exam guide;
- ships **only original** practice items, written from the published objectives;
- reproduces from the official guide only the **task statement titles**, which function as an index of what is tested, and cites the guide as their source.

Everything else — the explanations, the concept notes, the distractor taxonomy, the practice items — is original work by this project's contributors.

## If you have sat the exam

Do not contribute anything you saw on it. Not a paraphrase, not "a question a bit like this one", not a hint about which topics were heavily represented on your form. Doing so breaches your NDA, puts your credential at risk, and would force us to remove the contribution and possibly rewrite surrounding material.

Write from the published objectives and from your own production experience. That is what makes good practice material anyway.

## No guarantee

Passing the exam is your responsibility. This kit is a study aid built against a specific published guide version (v1.0, July 2026). The underlying technology moves quickly and the exam blueprint may change. Nothing here guarantees a passing result, and no contributor accepts liability for an exam outcome.

If you believe this repository contains material it should not — a rights concern, a suspected NDA breach in a contribution, or anything else — open an issue or contact the maintainers, and we will act on it promptly.
